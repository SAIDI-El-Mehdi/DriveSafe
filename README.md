# 🚗 DriveSafe - DriveAware

 DriveSafe (DriveAware) is an advanced intelligent system designed to enhance road safety by monitoring driver behavior and environmental conditions. By leveraging Computer Vision and Real-Time processing, it acts as a smart co-pilot to prevent accidents and ensure a safer driving experience.

✨ Key Features

*   👁️ **Driver Attention Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of the driver's gaze and head position to detect drowsiness or distraction.
*   🚦 **Smart Alerts:** Provides instant visual and auditory warnings when potential hazards are detected.
*   📊 **Trip Analytics:** Logs driving behavior data to provide post-trip safety reports and improvement suggestions.
